A product designer across
research, design, and code.

I’m Yuan, a product designer working across HCI research, interaction design, and front-end development. I study how people use technology, shape those interactions, and use code to simulate how they work.

My work spans wearables, apps, websites, and VR learning systems. Working across these formats has made me curious about what happens when an interface moves beyond a familiar screen: what people notice, what makes sense to them, and where they get stuck.

I’m currently designing for smart glasses. I also build the websites I design with Next.js and Tailwind, shaping how they look, feel, and work through both design and implementation.

My background combines a master’s in Networking and Multimedia with a focus on HCI from National Taiwan University and a bachelor’s in Interaction Design from Taipei Tech.

Based in Taipei, open to remote opportunities.
